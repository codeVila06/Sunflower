Welcome to Sunflower Park

Who We Are

Sunflower Park & Gardens is a real estate development company committed to making property ownership accessible, rewarding, and sustainable. We specialize in identifying high-potential land opportunities and transforming them into well-planned communities that offer both an exceptional living experience and long-term investment value.

With a focus on quality infrastructure, strategic locations, and customer-centric solutions, we provide individuals, families, and investors with the opportunity to own property through transparent processes and flexible payment options. Every development is designed to balance modern convenience, security, and green living, creating environments where people can live, grow, and thrive.

A Word from the CEO

Building a Legacy of Trust

At Sunflower Park, our vision extends beyond selling land or developing estates. We are committed to creating opportunities for individuals and families to secure their future through property ownership while building communities that people are proud to call home.

We understand that purchasing property is one of the most important decisions anyone can make. That is why we place great emphasis on transparency, quality, affordability, and trust in everything we do. Our goal is to simplify the journey to ownership by providing strategic investment opportunities, flexible payment solutions, and developments designed for long-term value.

Thank you for choosing to be part of our journey. We look forward to helping you take the next step toward ownership, growth, and lasting prosperity.

Warm regards, Ayodele Ayobolu Chief Executive Officer, Sunflower Park & Gardens
