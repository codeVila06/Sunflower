About Us

Sunflower Park & Gardens is a thoughtfully master-planned mixed-use real estate development company headquartered in Ikeja. We develop modern communities that combine quality infrastructure with generous green spaces, creating secure, healthy, and value-driven environments for residents and investors alike.

Designed with family living and long-term wealth creation in mind, Sunflower Park offers affordable land parcels in various plot sizes, alongside a diverse mix of residential and commercial opportunities. Our developments feature well-planned road networks, sustainable drainage systems, reliable infrastructure, and recreational amenities that enhance everyday living.
