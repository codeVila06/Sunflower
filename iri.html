About This Project

Project Overview

ÌRÌ by Sunflower is a thoughtfully planned residential community in Magboro, featuring bungalows, 3-bedroom terrace homes, semi-detached duplexes, and fully detached duplexes. Designed to foster community and modern living, the development offers 24/7 security, a fully equipped gym, a child-friendly park, a laundromat, and easy access to Lagos. With the C of O in view, ÌRÌ combines comfort, convenience, and long-term value in a secure and welcoming environment.
